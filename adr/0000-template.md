# ADR NNNN: Short Title in Title Case

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** Names of people who made the call
- **Supersedes:** ADR NNNN, or —
- **Superseded by:** ADR NNNN, or —
- **Related:** Other ADRs or spec files this touches

## Context

What is the situation that forced this decision? What constraints, requirements, or external pressures are in play? Reference the relevant spec files (e.g., `specs/02-content-metadata.md`) so future readers can trace the why.

Two or three short paragraphs. Be concrete; avoid generic "we need a database" framing.

## Decision

State the decision in one or two sentences, in **bold**, at the top of this section. Then expand with the concrete pieces — services, libraries, configurations, thresholds.

## Rationale

Why this decision over the alternatives? Lead with the strongest reasons. Connect each to a concrete need from Context. If the decision is a judgment call rather than a clear winner, say so.

## Alternatives considered

Each alternative gets a short paragraph naming what it is, why it was attractive, and why it lost. Do not strawman — if an alternative is genuinely competitive, say so honestly.

- **Alternative A.** What it is. Why considered. Why rejected.
- **Alternative B.** Same shape.

## Consequences

### Positive

- The good things this enables.

### Negative

- The downsides, lock-in, costs, and risks. Be honest. If a downside is material, name a mitigation.

### Follow-ups

- Decisions deferred to later ADRs.
- Operational tasks that need to happen before this decision is realized.
